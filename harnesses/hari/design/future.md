# Future options

These are deferred possibilities for the Hari harness under `harnesses/hari`.
They are not a roadmap, current contract, or implied authority to build them.
Reconsider only when concrete use shows that Hari's current local workflow needs
them.

## Coordination state

A compact decision-consumption journal and atomic amendments could make
cross-manager changes more explicit. Any design should keep shared Prime Radiant
records small and single-writer, preserve provenance, and avoid copied live-state
mirrors or a new platform by default.

## Continuity and workspaces

Exceptional child-session reassociation may be useful after a verified legitimate
manager continuation. It must preserve exact identity and writer protections.
Multi-checkout coordination may be added when a manager's approved work needs
concurrent non-overlapping writers. Neither option should create a project-manager
tier or replace native workspace lifecycle.

## Terminals

Linux terminal launching remains deferred.

## Persistent limits

Automatic manager waking, background coordination, GitHub publication, source
pushes, deployment, and live-system mutation are not current grants. Future work
must retain manager-owned acceptance, explicit authority, inspection of uncertain
outcomes before retry, and the distinction between conversation history and current records.
