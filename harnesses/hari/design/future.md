# Future options

These options are not current behavior or a roadmap. Reconsider them only when
actual use shows a need:

- A compact decision-consumption journal and atomic amendments for cross-manager
  changes. Keep shared records small and single-writer, with provenance.
- Exceptional child-session reassociation after verified legitimate continuation.
  Preserve exact identity and writer protections.
- Multi-checkout coordination for approved concurrent, non-overlapping work.
  Reuse native workspace lifecycle without another manager tier.
- Linux terminal launching.

The [operating model](harness-operating-model.md) remains binding. Autonomy and
automatic manager waking are never future options. New work does not grant
publication or live-system authority, or require a learning database,
prioritization model, or recovery framework.
